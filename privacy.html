Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Finance Documentary Automation ("the project") handles information when its owner uses the workflow.

Information and access

The workflow may access Google Drive files that the account owner chooses to make available through Google's authorization process. Depending on the workflow, this can include scripts, images, audio, video files, and related production materials.

How information is used

Information and files are used to support the owner's video-production workflow, including organizing production assets and creating documentary content. The project does not sell personal information.

Service providers

The workflow may rely on third-party services selected by its owner, such as cloud storage, content-generation, text-to-speech, and video-processing providers. Those services process information as needed to provide the requested functionality and are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and security

Files may be stored in the owner's Google Drive and other configured services. The owner is responsible for managing access permissions and protecting credentials. OAuth tokens and API keys should be stored securely and must not be published in this repository or on this website.

Sharing

The project does not intentionally make the owner's private Drive files public. Information may be processed by the third-party providers configured by the owner, or disclosed where required by law.

Data retention and deletion

Production files are retained in the storage locations chosen by the owner until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke Google account access through their Google Account security settings and can delete stored production files from the relevant services.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the workflow changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
